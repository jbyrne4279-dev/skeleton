Commercial rebuild cost assessments in London

On-site RICS rebuild cost assessments for London offices, retail, industrial and mixed-use buildings, from single sites to large portfolios. Desktop surveys are available for commercial property anywhere in the UK.

(001)

On-site surveys for London commercial property

Our surveyors inspect London commercial buildings in person. Restricted access, neighbouring buildings, party wall matters, conservation areas and the higher cost of building in the capital all affect the rebuild figure, and they are easy to miss without a site visit.

(002)

Why it matters for businesses

If a commercial building is under-insured, the average clause can reduce any claim in proportion, leaving the business to cover the shortfall. Rebuilding to current building regulations, with demolition and professional fees, often costs more than owners expect.

(003)

Buildings we assess

  • Multi-storey offices and headquarters buildings
  • Shops, retail units and shopping parades
  • Industrial units, warehouses and logistics sites
  • Hotels, leisure and hospitality buildings
  • Schools and other education buildings
  • Mixed-use and multi-let buildings
(004)

What the assessment covers

  • Structure, frame, roof and external envelope
  • Basements, car parks and podium levels
  • Building services, lifts and plant
  • Fit-out that forms part of the insured building
  • External works, parking and service yards
  • Demolition, site clearance, professional fees and VAT where applicable
(005)

Who we work with

  • Commercial property owners and investors
  • Asset and portfolio managers
  • Facilities and property managers
  • Landlords of multi-let buildings
  • Insurance brokers and their clients
(006)

Single sites and portfolios

We assess single buildings and whole London portfolios. Every site gets the same standard of report, so your broker has a consistent rebuild figure for each building you insure.
