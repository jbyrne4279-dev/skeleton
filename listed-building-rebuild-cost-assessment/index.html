Rebuild cost assessments for listed and heritage buildings

Specialist RICS rebuild cost assessments for listed, thatched and period properties, where matching materials and traditional trades raise the cost of rebuilding.

(001)

Why listed buildings cost more to rebuild

Repairs and rebuilding work to a listed building usually need listed building consent, and the local authority may require matching materials and traditional methods. Specialist trades, longer programmes and heritage professionals all add to the cost.

(002)

What we allow for

  • Traditional construction such as solid stone or brick, timber frame and cob
  • Natural stone, handmade bricks, lime mortar, clay tiles, slate and thatch
  • Sash windows, joinery, mouldings and decorative details
  • Specialist trades such as stonemasons, thatchers and lime plasterers
  • Conservation requirements and listed building consent
  • Demolition, site clearance, professional fees and VAT
(003)

Properties we assess

  • Listed buildings of every grade
  • Homes in conservation areas
  • Thatched cottages
  • Georgian, Victorian and Edwardian properties
  • Barn conversions and timber-framed buildings
(004)

Why a calculator is not enough

Rebuild calculators are based on standard modern construction. They do not reflect traditional materials, specialist labour or conservation rules, so they can seriously underestimate the rebuild cost of a listed or thatched property.
