Rebuild cost assessments for homes

A RICS rebuild cost assessment for your house, bungalow or flat. We work out what it would really cost to rebuild your home, so your buildings insurance covers it in full.

(001)

Why homeowners need one

Most home insurers ask you to set your own buildings sum insured. If that figure is too low, the average clause can reduce what you are paid on any claim, even for partial damage. A rebuild cost assessment replaces guesswork with a figure based on your actual home.

(002)

What we look at

  • The gross internal floor area of your home
  • Construction type, from brick and block to timber frame or stone
  • Roof structure, specification and finishes
  • Extensions, loft conversions, basements and conservatories
  • Garages, outbuildings, boundary walls and drives
  • Demolition, site clearance, professional fees and VAT
(003)

Homes we assess

  • Detached, semi-detached and terraced houses
  • Bungalows and chalet bungalows
  • Flats and maisonettes you insure yourself
  • Buy-to-let properties and HMOs
  • Period homes and non-standard construction
(004)

When to get one

  • When you buy a home or take out a new policy
  • Before renewal, if your sum insured has not been checked in three years
  • After an extension, loft conversion or major refurbishment
  • If your insurer or broker asks for a professional rebuild figure
