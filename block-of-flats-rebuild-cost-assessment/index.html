Rebuild cost assessments for blocks of flats in London

Whole-building RICS rebuild cost assessments for freeholders, residents’ management companies, RTM companies and managing agents, with on-site surveys across London and desktop surveys UK-wide.

(001)

Meet your duty to insure

Most residential leases require the landlord or management company to insure the building for its full reinstatement value. An up-to-date rebuild cost assessment helps you meet that obligation and shows leaseholders how the sum insured was reached.

(002)

What the assessment covers

  • The whole structure, including roofs and balconies
  • Communal entrances, stairs and corridors
  • Lifts, plant rooms and building services
  • Basements, car parks, bin and cycle stores
  • Boundary walls, gates, landscaping and external works
  • Demolition, site clearance, professional fees and VAT
(003)

Who we work with

  • Freeholders and landlords
  • Residents’ management companies (RMCs)
  • Right to manage (RTM) companies
  • Managing agents and block managers
  • Housing providers with several blocks
(004)

Keeping the figure current

RICS recommends a full rebuild cost assessment at least every three years, with the figure index-linked each year in between. Reassess sooner after major works such as a new roof, an extension or remediation works.
