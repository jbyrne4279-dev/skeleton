Rebuild cost assessments for every property

From a family home to a 200-unit residential block, we calculate the full cost to demolish, clear the site and rebuild. That figure is what your buildings insurance sum insured should be.

Houses, bungalows, flats and HMOs. A RICS-compliant rebuild valuation covering construction, demolition and site clearance, professional fees, and VAT where applicable. Suitable for homeowners and buy-to-let landlords.

Freeholders, managing agents, RMCs and RTM companies have a duty to insure correctly under the lease. We assess the whole building including communal areas, car parks, lifts and external works, with a report leaseholders can rely on.

Offices, retail, industrial units, warehouses, hotels, schools and mixed-use buildings. Elemental costings that reflect specification, plant and services so your business can rebuild properly after a loss.

Period, thatched and listed properties need like-for-like materials and specialist trades. Our surveyors account for conservation requirements and the true cost of traditional construction.

How it works

A simple process from enquiry to report.

  1. (001)

    Request a quote

    Tell us the postcode, property type and size. We'll confirm the right type of assessment for your property.

  2. (002)

    Assessment

    A RICS surveyor carries out a desktop assessment or a site inspection, measuring the gross internal area and recording construction and specification.

  3. (003)

    Costing

    We apply current BCIS rebuild cost data, regional adjustments, demolition, site clearance, professional fees and VAT.

  4. (004)

    Your report

    A signed rebuild cost assessment report to send to your insurer or broker and use to set your sum insured.
